THE IT CODE (Pty) Ltd is a South African software engineering and digital transformation company focused on developing practical technology solutions that improve how organisations operate, deliver services, and make decisions.

Our Story

The company was founded on the belief that technology should not exist simply as a tool, but as a solution to real operational challenges. Many organisations continue to experience inefficiencies caused by manual processes, fragmented information systems, and workflows that have not evolved with modern demands.

THE IT CODE exists to bridge this gap by engineering digital solutions that simplify processes, improve efficiency, and create measurable value.

What “THE IT CODE” Means

“IT” carries a dual meaning — Information Technology and the defining standard of excellence behind every solution. “CODE” represents the process of designing and building digital solutions that address operational challenges.

Together, THE IT CODE represents the quality code behind problem-solving technology — solutions that are reliable, scalable, and engineered with purpose.

THE PROBLEM

The hidden cost of inefficient workflows

Across South Africa, many organisations still depend on manual processes, paper-based records, spreadsheets and disconnected systems. These approaches create long waiting times, delayed reporting, limited visibility and increased pressure on staff.

Long waiting times

Patients and clients spend unnecessary time waiting because information and processes move too slowly.

Fragmented information

Critical data lives in multiple places, making it difficult to retrieve and act on quickly.

Administrative overload

Healthcare workers and staff spend too much time on paperwork instead of serving people.

Limited visibility

Leaders lack real-time operational insight, slowing decision-making and improvement.

FOUNDER

From experience to innovation

THE IT CODE was founded by Keorapetse Makhubo, a software engineer and ICT professional driven by the belief that technology should solve meaningful problems.

The journey began through direct observation of challenges in Northern Cape healthcare facilities. Patients experienced long waiting periods while staff managed paper-based records and manual processes. These experiences raised a simple question: How could technology reduce operational barriers and improve service delivery?

Drawing on experience in software development, systems analysis, database design, data analytics and digital transformation — and postgraduate research in Information and Communication Technology — HealthSync™ was born as a research-backed, real-world solution.
